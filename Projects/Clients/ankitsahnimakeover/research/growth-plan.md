# Sahni Beauty Salon Growth Domination Plan

Sahni Beauty Parlour already has the strongest trust base in Lahar: operated since 1994, open daily 10am–8pm, offers hair, skin, nails, hair removal, and makeup, holds 4.6 rating from 116 reviews on Justdial. Praval Makeover (Mihona) has built stronger premium digital presence with website, bridal positioning, 10 years messaging, and clearly marketed services like airbrush makeup, HD makeup, eyelash extensions, body polishing, and tiered pre-bridal packages.

## Executive Objective

Become the default beauty brand across a 100km radius by combining Sahni's local trust, service quality, and longevity with stronger premium positioning, better digital systems, and repeatable client acquisition.

## Current Position

### Existing Advantages

- 30+ year legacy and community trust since 1994
- 4.6 rating with 116 reviews — strong social proof in small-town market
- Broad service mix: hair, skin, nails, hair removal, makeup
- Daily opening hours, easy access
- 25K Instagram followers (@ankitsahnimakeovers)

### Current Weaknesses

- No public website (Praval has branded website)
- Premium bridal/makeover positioning weaker online
- Advanced premium services not clearly marketed
- No visible online booking funnel, review engine, or structured lead capture

## Competitor: Praval Makeover

**Strengths:** Premium website-led branding, bridal-first marketing, package architecture (Gold/Silver/Deluxe/O3+), HD & airbrush makeup, eyelash extensions, body polishing, advanced facials, nail extensions.

**Gaps:** Fewer reviews than Sahni, located in Mihona (not Lahar), smaller community footprint.

## 7-Phase Domination Strategy

### Phase 1: Win Local Search
- Create and fully optimize Google Business Profile
- Add exact categories: Beauty Parlour, Bridal Makeup, Facial Spa, Nail Salon, Hair Salon
- Upload 50+ high-quality photos: salon interior, before/after, bridal looks, team, hygiene
- Add service list with prices
- Publish 2 Google posts/week
- Collect 5–10 new Google reviews/week for 12 weeks
- Ask reviewers to mention: "best salon in Lahar", "bridal makeup in Lahar", "best beauty parlour in Bhind district"

### Phase 2: Premium Repositioning

Signature branded packages:
- Sahni Bridal Signature HD Package
- Sahni Royal Pre-Bridal Package
- Sahni Festival Glow Package
- Sahni Monthly Self-Care Membership
- Sahni Teen/College Fresh Look Package

Services to add or market more clearly:
- HD Makeup, Airbrush Makeup
- Nail extensions and gel polish
- Eyelash extensions
- Advanced facials by skin type
- Body polishing, Aroma massage
- Hair spa packages
- Keratin / smoothening / rebonding
- Bridal trials and consultation

### Phase 3: Conversion Machine

Lead path:
1. Instagram Reel or Google search
2. WhatsApp click
3. Instant auto-reply with menu + price + offer
4. Booking deposit or confirmation
5. Reminder message
6. Review request after visit
7. Repeat offer after 21 days

WhatsApp Business must include:
- Catalog with every service
- Bridal package PDF/images
- Quick replies, greeting message, away message
- Broadcast lists for old clients
- Labels: Bridal lead, Facial lead, VIP client, Pending follow-up, Repeat customer

### Phase 4: Content System

Weekly content mix:
- 3 Reels: bridal transformation, haircut transformation, skincare/facial result
- 2 carousels: package menu, customer testimonials
- 3 Stories/day: behind the scenes, slot availability, polls, reviews
- 1 local authority video/week: beauty tips in Hindi for brides, students, festival looks
- 1 founder video/week for trust and familiarity

Content angles: before/after, bridal countdown, festive looks, real testimonials, skin/hair education, "Why Sahni since 1994" legacy storytelling

### Phase 5: Referral and Retention Engine

- Refer 1 friend = free threading or ₹100 credit
- 5 visits = free service upgrade
- Bridal referral partner commission for photographers, mehendi artists, boutiques, wedding halls
- Birthday month facial discount
- Membership plans: monthly, quarterly, bridal season VIP
- Reactivation campaign for clients who haven't visited in 45–60 days

### Phase 6: Community Monopoly

- Beauty workshops in Lahar schools/colleges
- Sponsor local women's events, mehendi contests, Karwa Chauth/Teej beauty events
- Partner with local boutiques, jewellers, photographers, event decorators, coaching centers
- Create "Sahni Bridal Circle" vendor network across nearby towns
- Home service premium packages for brides within travel radius

### Phase 7: Radius Expansion to 100km

- First dominate Lahar + Mihona/Daboh with digital ads and reviews
- Then target Bhind, Gormi, Datia with geo-targeted Instagram/Facebook ads
- Build landing pages for each target town
- Promote travel bridal makeup packages
- Recruit/train one mobile bridal team
- Standardize SOPs, hygiene, uniforms, scripts, package names, pricing before adding branch

## Non-Negotiable Standards

- Fast reply time: under 5 minutes during business hours
- Consistent hygiene and premium presentation
- Uniform brand colors across salon, Instagram, WhatsApp, and print
- Every client asked for photo/testimonial permission
- Every bridal client gets consultation checklist
- Every repeat client gets personalized follow-up
