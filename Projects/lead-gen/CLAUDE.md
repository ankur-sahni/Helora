Build a lead generation system for the AI automation agency.

## Status
Planning phase. Target audience and platform TBD.

## Context
- This feeds clients into the agency ($30K MRR target)
- Cold email + LinkedIn outreach is the primary go-to-market channel
- Target: global clients billed in USD, not Indian SMBs in INR
- ICP: B2B SaaS ($1M-$20M ARR) OR recruiting/staffing agencies (20-150 employees)
- Volume target: 1,500-2,000 cold touches/week to produce 8-15 calls/week

## Planned Stack
- Lead sourcing: Apollo.io
- Email sending: Instantly or Smartlead (2-3 warmed domains)
- LinkedIn outreach: Heyreach or LaGrowthMachine
- Booking: Cal.com
- Demo: Loom
- Orchestration: n8n
