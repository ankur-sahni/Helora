# Ankit Sahni Makeover — Project Context

> Local context for this project. Global agency rules apply from parent CLAUDE.md.

## Client
- **Business:** Sahni Beauty Salon (brand name: Ankit Sahni Makeover)
- **Location:** Lahar, Madhya Pradesh, India
- **Owners:** Ankit Sahni & Kusum Sahni
- **Established:** 1994 (30+ years, oldest and most trusted salon in area)
- **Photographer credit:** @ankur_sahni

## Services
- Hair: cutting, styling, coloring, straightening, spa
- Makeup: bridal (primary), engagement, party
- Skin: facials (Hydra Facial), bleach, skincare
- Nails: nail extensions, manicure/pedicure, threading, waxing
- Rentals: bridal lehenga, bridal jewelry, party wear lehengas & gowns

## Current Situation
- Strong offline presence, zero digital presence
- No website, no online booking, no social media automation
- Largest bridal rental collection in the area

## Deliverables
1. Logo (brand identity)
2. Website
3. Online booking system
4. AI chatbot (WhatsApp-first for MP market)
5. Instagram / social media automation
6. Full marketing strategy

## Key Constraints
- Strategy must be practical and low-cost to run
- Hindi/English bilingual where needed
- Simple enough for a non-tech owner to manage day-to-day
- WhatsApp is the primary channel for this market (not Instagram — Instagram is top-of-funnel only)
- Instagram Reels are highest-leverage content channel

## Target Audience
- Brides and wedding clients (primary)
- Women and young girls
- Regular grooming customers
- Clients from nearby towns

## Vision
- #1 beauty brand in the region
- Premium bridal studio brand
- Future: expansion to nearby cities + beauty training academy
