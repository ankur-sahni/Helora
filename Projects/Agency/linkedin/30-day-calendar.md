## Writing Rules

- Under 200 words per post.
- Hook in first line. Never lead with "I've been thinking about…".
- One idea per post. Two = reduces recall.
- End with an open question or a specific claim — not "what do you think?"
- Numbers over adjectives. "1,200 followers in 30 days" > "strong growth".
- Admit mistakes explicitly. "I was wrong about X" outperforms generic confession posts.
- No emojis in the body (one is OK in a title if natural). Indian founder audience reads emojis as sales-y.
- Every Friday = a numbers post. Non-negotiable.

---

## Engagement Rules

- Reply to every comment within 24 hours in the first 30 days.
- Engage on 10 posts from Tier 1 audience each day (Indian founders, Indian agencies).
- Do not argue in comments. If someone is wrong, engage once with a source, then leave.
- Weekends = comment day. No original posts. Deep engagement on 20 posts Sat + Sun.
