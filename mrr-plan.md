# MRR Plan — Honest 12-Month Roadmap

**Owner:** Ankur Sahni
**Last updated:** 2026-04-18
**Purpose:** Replace the aspirational "$30k MRR in 12 months" target with a grounded, week-by-week plan that an operator-of-one can actually execute.

---

## TL;DR — The Honest Answer

**$30k MRR is an 18–24 month target, not a 12-month target.**

The 12-month honest ceiling is **$6,500–$8,000 MRR** assuming:
- Sahni Bridal Studio becomes a credible case study by end of Month 2.
- WhatsApp outbound to MP-UP salons converts at ~3% after Month 3.
- LinkedIn build-in-public produces 1–2 global clients by Month 6.
- ReviewLoop micro-SaaS launches in Month 4 and reaches 40 users by Month 12.
- Ankur does not burn out and does not chase shiny new verticals.

If any one of those fails, the 12-month number drops to **$3,500–$4,500 MRR**.

## Core Assumptions

| Variable | Assumption | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary product | Salon AI Growth Stack | WhatsApp receptionist + IG content engine + Google Review bot |
| Primary price | ₹20,000/month (~$240) | Below ₹15k margin collapses; above ₹25k Tier-3 salons balk |

| Sales cycle (warm referral) | 2 weeks | Brother-of-salon-owner intro, IG DM with Sahni proof |

| Monthly churn (M1–M6) | 15% | Indian SMBs cancel fast; ROI must be visible in 30 days |
| Monthly churn (M7–M12) | 8% | Better qualification + guarantee filters tyre-kickers |
| Capacity ceiling | 8–10 active clients solo | After that: VA needed or service breaks |
| USD conversion | ₹83 = $1 | Locked for planning; actual FX will vary |

## What Has to Be True for This Plan to Work

1. **Sahni becomes a 10-numbers case study by end of M2.** Followers, WhatsApp conversations, bookings, revenue uplift, reviews added. If Sahni doesn't produce numbers, the whole plan slips 2–3 months.
2. **Ankur does outbound every single day.** 20 cold WhatsApps/day, 5 LinkedIn posts/week. Non-negotiable.
3. **Churn stays under 15% early, under 10% later.** One bad delivery month destroys 2 months of acquisition.
4. **No new verticals until M10.** Salons only. Clinics and coaching centers are Phase 4.
5. **ReviewLoop must not become a side project that steals salon focus.** It is a leveraged income stream, not the main business.
6. **Ankur hires a VA by M7.** Without this, capacity caps MRR at ~$3k forever.

---

## What Kills This Plan

- Chasing B2B SaaS globally before Indian case studies land (prior outreach agent bug).
- Going below ₹15k/month to "land the first client" — destroys pricing anchor for future clients.
- Over-customising each client instead of enforcing a productised template.
- Building a 4th product before ReviewLoop hits 50 users.
- Treating LinkedIn as optional. Without inbound, only Indian market = lower ceiling.
